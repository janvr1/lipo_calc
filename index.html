How is this calculated?

Everything is converted to energy (watt-hours), since the two batteries usually have different voltages:

Field Wh = V × Ah × usable%
Delivered Wh = Field Wh × charger efficiency
Pack Wh per charge = cells × cell V × Ah × used%
Charges = Delivered Wh ÷ Pack Wh

It's an estimate: cold weather, high charge rates, a charger that steps voltage up (field voltage lower than pack voltage) and older batteries all reduce the real number.
